Privacy & Cookie Policy

Jelgava Coworking Space (Kazarmes iela 16B, Jelgava, Latvia)

We respect your privacy. This page explains how we process personal data and use cookies.

What data do we collect?

  • Contact form: name, email address, phone (optional), message.
  • Visit data: Google Tag Manager (technical visit data for website operation).

What we do not do?

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use advertising cookies (Google Ads, etc.).
  • We do not process sensitive personal data.

Cookies

We only use functional cookies required for the website to work (e.g. your chosen language). Google Tag Manager is used for visit statistics without personal identification.

Data retention

Form data is stored in Formspree and used only to contact you. You have the right to request deletion of your data — write to konstantin@jelgavacoworking.lv.
