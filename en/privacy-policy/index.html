Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Jelgava Coworking Space (Kazarmes iela 16B, Jelgava, LV-3001, Latvia) respects your privacy. This page explains what data we collect, how we use cookies, and what rights you have.

What data we collect

  • Contact form on the website: name, email address, phone (optional), message.
  • Technical visit data via Google Tag Manager (e.g. which pages are viewed).
  • With your consent — ad measurement data via the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel, to understand how effective our ad campaigns are.

Cookies

We use two types of cookies:

  • Necessary (functional) — always active, required for the site to work (e.g. remembering your cookie choice). No consent is required for these.
  • Marketing cookies — only set if you consent. We use the Meta Pixel to measure the performance of our Instagram and Facebook ads. These cookies are set by Meta Platforms Ireland Limited.

You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” button in the footer of the page.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not process sensitive personal data.
  • We do not set marketing cookies without your prior consent.

Data retention

Contact form data is stored with Formspree and used only to get in touch with you.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. Write to konstantin@jelgavacoworking.lv.
